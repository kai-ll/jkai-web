🗑️ StepPot Account & Data Deletion Guide

Last updated: October 31, 2025

How to Delete Your Account

You can delete your StepPot account directly from within the app. Once your account is deleted, all related data will be permanently removed and cannot be recovered.

  1. Open the StepPot app.
  2. Go to Settings → My Information.
  3. Tap the Delete Account button.
  4. Confirm the deletion when prompted.

After confirmation, your account and all associated data (such as step history, rewards, and tickets) will be permanently deleted from our servers within a short period. You will receive an in-app confirmation once the deletion is complete.

Data Deleted Upon Account Removal

When you delete your account, the following data will be removed:

  • Account profile information (name, email, user ID)
  • Step tracking history
  • Rewards, tickets, and lottery entries
  • Friends or invite relationships
  • Any associated app preferences

Once deleted, this data cannot be restored.
